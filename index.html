About US

Active Dad's is a Non-Profit Organisation (NPO) committed to building a healthy and sustainable environment for now and the future generation. Our vision is simple: having a better community for all who lives in it. We work to achieve this through two main objectives. First, we provide educational support to children, giving them the guidance and encouragement they need to succeed. Second, we promote community development in our area through sports, arts and culture, environmental education, and educational tours and exploration. As an NPO, we are guided by transparency and accountability. We keep strict records of everything we own, we do not give money or property to members or office bearers except as reasonable payment for work actually done for the organisation, and no member holds any personal right to the property or assets of the organisation.
